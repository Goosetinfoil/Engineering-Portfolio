Who I Am

About Me

I'm a second-year Mechatronics Engineering Technology student at Rochester Institute of Technology, interested in robotics and automation. I'm drawn to projects that sit at the intersection of mechanical design and code — CAD models that become real parts, and control loops that make hardware actually respond to the world.

My interest in this space started early — building an Arduino-powered car during COVID and competing in robotics through high school — and it's carried through into how I approach engineering now: practical, iterative, and hands-on.
